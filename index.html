What We Analyze

📏
Face Dimensions
Measures face width, length, forehead, jaw, and cheekbone widths in millimetres using landmark ratios.
🔮
Face Shape Detection
Classifies into Oval, Round, Square, Heart, Diamond, or Oblong based on proportional analysis.
👁️
Eye Analysis
Calculates eye width, height, aspect ratio, and detects Almond, Round, Hooded, Upturned, or Downturned shape.
↔️
Pupillary Distance
Precisely computes interpupillary distance (PD) — critical for accurate lens placement in spectacle frames.
👓
Frame Recommendation
Recommends optimal frame size (S/M/L) and style (Rectangle, Round, Aviator, Rimless etc.) for your face.
📟
ESP32 OLED Display
Results are sent via WiFi to an ESP32 microcontroller and displayed on an SSD1306 OLED screen in rotating slides.
